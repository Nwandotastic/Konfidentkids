Section 01 · System

One template. Eight pages. A hundred songs.

Each master template is a single Canva video design with eight pages. Four of those pages are unique layouts — title, verse, chorus, outro. The other four are duplicates. To make a new video, you swap the lyric text, swap the illustration, replace the audio, and resize. The brand, the rhythm, and the polish carry over automatically. The 9:16 master is your TikTok / Reels / Shorts engine. The 16:9 master is your YouTube long-form engine. Together they cover every distribution surface in the launch playbook.

★ The single rule that makes this template valuable

Resist the urge to "improve" the template per video. The whole point is consistency. Same intro card. Same outro card. Same lyric text style. Same animation timing. The look becomes recognisable only when you stop fiddling. Treat the template like a recipe, not a sketch.

A
Template A · the 9:16 vertical master.

For TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Eight pages, four unique layouts. Total runtime ~50–60 seconds — the optimal length for short-form completion rates and algorithmic reach on a brand-new account.

1080 × 1920 px 8 pages · 4 unique layouts ~55s runtime
Page 01 · Title 1.5s

Cold-open title card.

A scroll-stopper. Yellow gradient + the "Rhyme Time ♪" lockup tells parents within half a second that this is your channel. Builds recognition across all videos.

  1. Background: add a rectangle, fill with gradient — top #FFE88A to bottom #FFD23F.
  2. Decorative dots: two off-canvas circles in KK White at 30–40% opacity, top-left and bottom-right corners.
  3. Title text: "Rhyme Time" in Fraunces Italic 120pt, KK Blue (#1E5BBA), centered.
  4. Music note ♪ in DM Sans 90pt, KK Navy, directly below.
  5. Brand wordmark: "KONFIDENT KIDS" in DM Sans 700 18pt, letter-spacing 2px, KK Navy at 70% opacity, below the note.
  6. Animation: apply Pan entry to the background, Pop entry to the title text. Set page duration to 1.5s.
Pages 02 / 04 / 06 · Verse 8–12s each

Verse slide.

The workhorse layout. Illustration on top half, lyrics on bottom half. You'll duplicate this page once per verse and only swap the illustration + the text. The structure never changes.

  1. Background: soft pastel gradient — KK White top to a rotating accent (sky / mint / peach / lavender) bottom. Match the song's mood.
  2. Illustration zone (top): rounded rectangle frame, 136mm × 120mm equivalent, centred — drop in a 3D character or scene from Canva's library here. Frame stays. Image inside swaps.
  3. Lyric block (middle-bottom): 2–3 lines of Fraunces Italic 72pt, KK Navy, centred. The last line of each verse is bumped up to 80pt and coloured KK Blue for emphasis.
  4. Bottom brand bar: 4mm yellow underline + "KONFIDENT KIDS" in DM Sans 700 16pt at 60% opacity. Same on every verse page.
  5. Animation: background Drift, illustration Rise, lyric block Typewriter word-by-word. Page duration matches verse length in audio.
  6. Duplicate this page twice (becomes pages 4 and 6). Only the illustration and text change between duplicates.
Pages 03 / 05 / 07 · Chorus 8s each

Chorus slide.

High-energy contrast to the verse. Yellow background, decorative rays, illustration enlarged, chorus text dropped in big with word-by-word entrance animation. This is where children sing along — make it bouncy.

  1. Background: yellow diagonal gradient — #FFD23F to #FFC300. Bold, saturated.
  2. Decorative rays: six thin white lines at 25% opacity radiating from upper-third centre (use Canva's "Lines" element, duplicate, rotate).
  3. Illustration: larger version of the verse character — frame is 160mm circle, centred upper third. Same character, bigger presence.
  4. Chorus lyrics: Fraunces Italic weight 600, 76pt, KK Navy. Centred. 2–3 lines.
  5. "SING ALONG ♪" badge: DM Sans 700 22pt, letter-spacing 2px, KK Navy. Bottom-centred, above safe zone.
  6. Animation: background Pan, illustration Bounce, chorus text Pop word-by-word. Higher energy than verse.
  7. Duplicate twice (becomes pages 5 and 7). The illustration grows in scale slightly each time — page 3 = 100%, page 5 = 110%, page 7 = 115%.
Page 08 · Outro 3s

Outro + CTA.

The funnel moment. Drives comments (which boost reach) and tells parents how to claim the Power Phrase Cards lead magnet. Identical on every video — no exceptions.

  1. Background: solid KK Navy #0B1A3A with two soft yellow radial-gradient circles in the corners at 8–10% opacity (matches your playbook hero).
  2. Logo block: upload your KK logo or build a placeholder — yellow rounded square 240×240px, "KK" in Fraunces Italic 600 at 130pt, KK Navy.
  3. Wordmark: "Konfident Kids" in Fraunces Italic 500, 70pt, KK White, below the logo.
  4. CTA button: KK Yellow rounded rectangle. Text "COMMENT 'BRAVE'" in DM Sans 700 44pt, letter-spacing 1px, KK Navy. Subline "for free Power Phrase Cards" in DM Sans 600 32pt below.
  5. Secondary line: "SUBSCRIBE FOR NEW RHYMES ♪" in DM Sans 600 24pt, letter-spacing 2px, KK Yellow at 85% opacity, near bottom safe zone.
  6. Animation: logo block Pop, CTA Slide-up. Hold for full 3s. This page never changes between videos.
★ Save as Brand Template — not as a Design

Once Template A is built, hit the ... menu → Save as Brand Template. Brand Templates lock the structure so accidental edits don't break your eight-page system. Every new video starts as Use template from this saved version — never from a copied design. This is the single biggest time-saver in the whole system.

B
Template B · the 16:9 horizontal master.

For YouTube long-form. Same eight-page structure as Template A, but each layout is reflowed to landscape proportions — verse uses a 60/40 illustration-to-lyric split rather than vertical stacking. This is the version that earns you organic search traffic on YouTube Kids long-tail.

1920 × 1080 px 8 pages · 4 unique layouts ~55s runtime
Page 01 · Title 1.5s

Cold-open title card.

Same brand as Template A but reflowed for the wider canvas — "Rhyme Time" runs as a single line, larger, more cinematic. YouTube viewers expect a slightly more produced opening than TikTok.

  1. Background: diagonal gradient — top-left #FFE88A to bottom-right #FFD23F.
  2. Decorative dots: top-left and bottom-right corner circles in KK White at 30–40%, larger than 9:16 versions.
  3. Title: "Rhyme Time" in Fraunces Italic 180pt — single line, KK Blue, centred.
  4. Music note + wordmark: ♪ in 110pt KK Navy, then "KONFIDENT KIDS" in DM Sans 700 28pt letter-spacing 2px below.
  5. Animation: Pan background, Pop title. Page duration 1.5s.
Pages 02 / 04 / 06 · Verse 8–12s each

Verse slide.

60/40 split: illustration on the left two-thirds, lyrics stacked on the right third. Eyes naturally read left → right, so viewers see the character first then read along to the lyric. Better for older kids who are reading.

  1. Background: horizontal gradient — left KK Sky #D9EFFF (or rotating accent) to right KK White.
  2. Left illustration zone: rounded rectangle 1140 × 920px, left margin 96px, vertically centred. Drop in 3D character or scene.
  3. Right lyric stack: Fraunces Italic 64pt, KK Navy, left-aligned. Last line of each verse bumps to 72pt + KK Blue.
  4. Bottom brand bar: 2mm full-width yellow line at 50% opacity + "KONFIDENT KIDS · RHYME TIME" wordmark in DM Sans 700 18pt at 60% opacity, bottom-left.
  5. Animation: illustration Rise, lyrics Typewriter word-by-word at slightly slower pace than 9:16 (YouTube viewers have higher attention).
  6. Duplicate twice for verses 2 and 3 (pages 4 and 6).
Pages 03 / 05 / 07 · Chorus 8s each

Chorus slide.

Centred composition for the chorus — illustration becomes the focal point and chorus text drops in below. Yellow background still does the heavy lifting on energy. The shift from split-screen verse to centred chorus is the visual rhythm of the video.

  1. Background: diagonal yellow gradient (same as 9:16). Add four white rays at 22% opacity radiating from upper-third centre.
  2. Centre illustration: 800px circle frame, vertically centred upper third, illustration scaled to fill.
  3. Chorus lyrics: Fraunces Italic 600 weight, 88pt, KK Navy, centred below illustration. 1–2 lines maximum.
  4. "SING ALONG ♪" badge: DM Sans 700 28pt letter-spacing 2px, KK Navy, bottom-centred above safe zone.
  5. Animation: illustration Bounce, chorus text Pop word-by-word. Same as 9:16 but with longer dwell on the centred composition.
  6. Duplicate twice (pages 5 and 7), illustration scales 100% / 105% / 110% across the three chorus pages.
Page 08 · Outro 3–5s

Outro + subscribe CTA.

Landscape outro has room for both the lead-magnet CTA and an explicit subscribe prompt — YouTube rewards "tap to subscribe" prompts in long-form. Logo lockup sits left, CTA stack sits right, subscribe arrow points to YouTube's subscribe button position.

  1. Background: solid KK Navy with two soft yellow corner circles at 8–10% opacity (matches 9:16 outro).
  2. Left logo lockup: KK logo 480×480px yellow block + "Konfident Kids" wordmark in Fraunces Italic 500 84pt KK White, stacked vertically, left-anchored.
  3. Right CTA stack: KK Yellow rounded rectangle, "COMMENT 'BRAVE'" in DM Sans 700 60pt KK Navy + "for free Power Phrase Cards" subline in DM Sans 600 36pt.
  4. Secondary subscribe line: "SUBSCRIBE ♪ NEW RHYMES WEEKLY" in DM Sans 600 32pt letter-spacing 2px, KK Yellow at 85%.
  5. Tertiary "↓ TAP TO SUBSCRIBE ↓" in DM Sans 700 24pt KK White at 70% opacity, bottom-anchored — points eyes toward the YouTube UI subscribe button below the player.
  6. Animation: logo Pop, CTA Slide-up, subscribe text Fade-in with 0.5s delay. Page hold 5s — longer dwell on YouTube than TikTok.
★ Don't rebuild — duplicate and reflow

The fastest way to build Template B isn't to build it from scratch. Build Template A first, save it as a Brand Template, then duplicate it and use Canva's Magic Switch to convert the entire eight-page design to 16:9. You'll then spend 10–15 minutes nudging text positions and resizing illustration frames to suit landscape — not rebuilding every layer. The Brand Kit colours, fonts, and animations all carry over.

Section 05 · The Per-Video Workflow

Once both templates are saved, every new video follows four steps.

From "I have a finished Suno track" to "video is rendering for export" — roughly 60–90 minutes per song, dropping to 45 minutes by your fifth video as muscle memory kicks in.

01

Use template

Open Template A from Brand Templates, click Use template. A fresh copy lands in your Designs.

02

Drop the track

Upload the Suno WAV via the Audio panel. Drag onto the timeline. Apply Beat Sync — Canva auto-aligns your eight pages to the song's beats.

03

Swap content

Replace the placeholder lyric text on every verse + chorus page. Replace the illustration in each frame using Canva's library or Magic Media. Brand bar stays.

04

Resize & export

Hit Magic Switch → 16:9 to generate the YouTube version. Nudge text positions on any page that misaligned. Export both as MP4 1080p.

★ Beat Sync is the secret weapon

Without Beat Sync, you'll spend an hour timing page transitions to the song manually. With Beat Sync, Canva does it in three clicks. Drop the track on the timeline → click the audio bar → select Beat Sync. Canva analyses the song's rhythm and snaps your page durations to musical beats. The first time you use it, it feels like cheating.

★ When the library doesn't have it, generate it

Canva Pro includes Magic Media (text-to-image) inside the editor. When the stock library can't find your shot, prompt Magic Media with: "3D Pixar-style cartoon illustration of [subject], soft lighting, pastel background, kids book aesthetic, no text". Five generations gets you to one usable image roughly 80% of the time. Save winners to a "KK Asset Library" folder so you can reuse them across videos.

Section 07 · Quality Gates

Three tests before you hit publish.

A finished video file isn't a finished video. Run these three checks every time. They take five minutes and will catch the issues that separate "this looks pro" from "this looks AI-generated".

GATE 01

The mute test.

Watch the whole video with sound off. Does the visual rhythm still feel intentional? Can you read every lyric? If the muted version feels confusing, the audio is doing too much heavy lifting — fix the visuals.

GATE 02

The phone test.

Open the export on your phone, not your laptop. Hold it at arm's length. Are the lyrics readable? Is the illustration clear at thumbnail size? Most viewers will see this video on a 6-inch screen — design for that, not your monitor.

GATE 03

The brand test.

Open this video alongside one of your finished Katy modules. Do they feel like the same brand? If your nursery rhyme video looks 30% different from your course content, something has drifted. Brand consistency is what builds a recognisable channel.
